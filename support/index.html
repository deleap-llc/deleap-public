PRODUCT HELP

How can we help?

For Kandy Labs product support, contact sweet.kandy@gmail.com.

Include the app name, Android or iOS version, app version and a description of the problem. Remove personal information from screenshots before sending them.

For account requests, see account-deletion instructions.

Keep your information private

Never send passwords, one-time sign-in codes or payment-card details. Do not post account details, family information or location data in public GitHub issues.
