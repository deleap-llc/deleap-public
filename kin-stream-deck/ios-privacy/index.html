KIN STREAM DECK · DELEAP LLC

iOS privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy covers the Kin Stream Deck iPhone and iPad app (com.kinstreamdeck.app), its companion connection, and the online services described below. DeLeap LLC operates Kin Stream Deck. For privacy questions or requests, email sweet.kandy@gmail.com.

No Kin account is required for ordinary local pairing and computer control. Optional online features and purchases involve additional processing. Kin Stream Deck is an independent productivity tool and is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or Elgato.

Pairing, saved decks and local computer control

The app stores paired-computer details, pairing credentials, deck information and preferences on your device. Pairing credentials are held in iOS Keychain storage. Your computer companion also stores its pairing credentials and authoritative decks. Discovery and control exchange computer capabilities, deck information, commands and acknowledgements over your local network. Pairing uses an encrypted connection, certificate verification and a revocable pairing token.

Local control does not require sending your deck commands to our cloud service. Optional voice and AI features can send text and button names to online services as explained below. Do not share pairing PINs, QR codes, tokens or private deck contents.

Camera access is used to scan the computer's pairing QR code. Scanning is processed on the device; the app does not upload camera images for pairing. Pairing links can contain the computer address and certificate fingerprint in a URL fragment. The fragment is not included in the website's HTTP request, although a browser or scanner may retain the link locally.

Installation identifiers, purchases and credits

The app generates a random installation identifier and keeps it in Keychain. It is used as an app user identifier for purchase entitlements, AI-service access, trial and credit accounting. It is a persistent identifier, not a name or an assurance that records are anonymous. Keychain information can survive app removal or be restored with device data.

Apple handles App Store checkout and payment details. We do not receive your payment-card number. The app, RevenueCat and our service process the installation identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase history, subscription status and related verification information to provide and restore eligible entitlements, report subscriptions and prevent fraud or duplicate credit grants. Purchase verification and subscription reporting are separate from the optional product-analytics choice below.

Our Microsoft Azure-hosted billing and credit service maintains installation-derived identifiers, entitlement and trial dates, credit balances and transaction/redemption records. These records are separate from local phone storage. Removing the app or forgetting a computer does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete server-side purchase records. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

Optional AI authoring and voice input

When you request AI authoring, text prompts, relevant computer capabilities and your installation identifier are sent over HTTPS to our hosted service and its configured AI provider. Voice matching sends the transcript and relevant existing button names so the service can find a matching control; it can also offer to draft a new action. Do not put passwords, secrets or other sensitive information into prompts or button names used for voice matching.

Voice capture uses the microphone and Apple's speech-recognition framework. Recognition may involve Apple's services depending on device and system capabilities. The app sends the resulting text, not the microphone audio, to our AI gateway. Apple's processing is subject to its own settings and privacy practices.

Our AI gateway is hosted on Microsoft Azure and forwards requests to its configured AI provider. Hosting, security and model providers may process operational or abuse-monitoring records under their applicable practices. We do not promise zero provider retention.

If you are given a managed evaluation account, submitting its sign-in details sends them to the evaluation service for verification. The app keeps the resulting evaluation session in memory; it does not persist that session across process termination.

Optional product analytics

Product analytics is off by default. The app offers this choice only after adult eligibility (18 or older) is established and requires affirmative consent. If an age is entered for this choice, the app retains the eligibility category rather than the entered age. Skipping the question, declining consent or having unknown or under-18 eligibility leaves optional analytics off.

If you opt in, Google Analytics for Firebase processes an app-instance identifier, app and device information, session information, approximate geography derived from network information, standard SDK events and our limited feature-use events. We use this information to understand reliability and feature usage. Our custom event schema excludes raw prompts, deck labels and contents, pairing secrets, email addresses and raw age. Custom-event limits do not describe all of Google's internal SDK processing or batching.

Advertising consent and advertising-related collection are disabled in the app's configuration; the app uses the Analytics Core variant without advertising-ID support. We do not sell app data or use it for advertising. These settings do not make analytics identifier-free. See Firebase privacy information and Google's data-collection controls.

You can withdraw consent using the app's analytics controls. Withdrawal disables future optional collection through the app; it does not automatically delete information already received by Google. Contact us to request deletion. The choice does not disable the online requests you initiate, purchase verification, newsletter delivery or providers' necessary operational processing. The iOS widget does not initialize Firebase Analytics.

Optional product-update emails

The pairing screen can save an editable email draft in local app preferences. Typing alone does not send it. Confirming pairing with a non-empty email separately requests a confirmation email from our newsletter service. Leave it blank to skip; signup failure does not prevent pairing.

Our newsletter service and Brevo process the address for confirmation and product updates. You join the newsletter only after confirming the email link. The signup request does not send your pairing credentials or deck contents, and the address is not used to grant paid access. Read the email privacy notice for unsubscribe and deletion information. Clearing a local draft does not unsubscribe an address already registered.

Providers and operational information

Apple, RevenueCat, Microsoft Azure and our configured AI provider, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Brevo process information for the features described above. Public documentation is hosted on GitHub Pages. Providers may receive IP addresses and other connection or operational information when you use their services. App support messages contain the information you choose to send. Do not post private support, purchase or deletion details in public GitHub issues.

We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service and its users. We use encrypted transport for paired computer control and cloud requests, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local deck information and preferences remain until removed through the relevant controls or app-data removal. Keychain records may remain after uninstalling. Forgetting a computer removes its saved pairing from the phone; use the companion's pairing controls when you also want to revoke computer-side access. Phone and computer copies of data have separate lifecycles.

Billing and credit records are kept to maintain entitlements, balances and duplicate-redemption protection; no fixed automatic expiry is promised. Newsletter records are kept while subscribed, and suppression records may remain after unsubscribe to prevent accidental re-enrollment. Analytics and other providers retain records according to the applicable service configuration and policies. Withdrawing analytics consent or uninstalling the app does not itself delete provider-held records.

For access, correction or deletion, email sweet.kandy@gmail.com and identify Kin Stream Deck for iOS. Requests are handled manually. We may ask for information to locate the relevant installation, subscription or newsletter record and verify your authority. Share purchase/order information only through private support; never send passwords, card numbers, pairing PINs or tokens.

We may retain records required for legal obligations, security, outstanding purchase disputes or necessary transaction integrity and will explain applicable limitations. A deletion request to us does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete records Apple independently maintains. Ordinary Stream Deck use does not create the shared Kin family-app account; this policy's request process applies to Stream Deck data.

Permissions, children and changes

Local Network permission supports discovery and communication with your computer. Camera, microphone and speech permissions support the features you initiate; you can manage them in iOS Settings. Computer-input permissions such as macOS Accessibility are granted on the companion computer, not on the iPhone.

Kin Stream Deck is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Optional product analytics is unavailable to under-18 or unknown-age users. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We will update this policy and its effective date when our practices change.
