iPhone and Mac details

Get started

  1. Install the desktop companion and the phone app using the links above.
  2. Open both apps and follow their pairing instructions.
  3. Tap a button on your phone and watch your desktop respond. Your pairing is remembered, so the apps reconnect when you reopen them.

See the installation and troubleshooting guide for the full setup.

Optional product updates

The phone pairing screen offers optional email updates. Pairing works without an email. Confirm your address by email to join; unsubscribe anytime. Read how newsletter signup uses your email.

Help and documentation

For help, contact sweet.kandy@gmail.com. Include the phone and desktop app versions, operating-system versions, and the step that failed. Do not send pairing codes, QR codes, passwords or private deck contents.
