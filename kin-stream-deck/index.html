Try it together

  1. Choose the available desktop companion and phone test app above, and install them using those links.
  2. Open both apps and follow their pairing instructions.
  3. Choose a harmless action on your phone and check that your desktop responds. Test reconnecting after closing and reopening the apps.

See the installation and troubleshooting guide for the full setup.
