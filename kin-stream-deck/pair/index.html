Pair your phone with your computer

Kin Stream Deck turns your phone into a control deck for your Windows or Mac computer.

Get the Android or iPhone test app

  1. Install and open Kin Stream Deck on your phone.
  2. Keep your phone and computer on the same local network.
  3. Scan the computer's QR code again, then enter the PIN shown on your computer.
App already installed?

If your camera did not open the app, open Kin Stream Deck and choose Scan PC QR code on Android or Scan pairing QR code on iPhone. For an older app, expand Compatibility QR for older apps on your computer and scan that code.

You will need to scan again after installation. This page does not save your pairing details.
