Product updates and the newsletter are optional. Leave the email field blank to pair without signing up.

What happens when you provide an email

The phone saves an editable email draft in encrypted local storage, separately from your paired computers. Typing or changing the draft does not send it. When you confirm pairing with an email filled in, the app independently requests a newsletter confirmation email. Pairing continues even if signup fails.

Our newsletter service sends the address to Brevo, our email provider. Confirm the link in the email to join the Kin Stream Deck newsletter list. An app message asking you to check your inbox is not confirmation that you are subscribed.

How we use it

DeLeap LLC uses confirmed addresses for Kin Stream Deck product updates and newsletter messages. Your address is not sent to your computer companion, included in app analytics, or used to decide access to paid features. The newsletter request does not include a pairing PIN, QR code, computer address, deck contents, or purchase token.

Brevo processes email delivery and subscription records. We do not request personal open or click tracking for the confirmation email. Our signup service temporarily uses an address-derived hash to limit repeated requests; it does not log email request bodies.

Unsubscribe or delete

Use the unsubscribe link in any newsletter to stop future newsletter mail. Changing or clearing the draft on your phone does not unsubscribe an older address. We keep subscription records while subscribed; an unsubscribe suppression record may remain to prevent accidental re-enrollment.

For access, correction, or deletion of your newsletter records, contact sweet.kandy@gmail.com from the address concerned. Please do not include pairing codes or passwords.
