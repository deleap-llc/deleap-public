Android privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. This policy covers the Kin Stream Deck Android app (com.kandylabs.kinstreamdeck), its AI and billing services, and optional product-update signup. DeLeap LLC publishes the app under the Sweet Kandy Google Play developer account.

Kin Stream Deck is an independent productivity tool. It is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, or Elgato. Privacy contact: sweet.kandy@gmail.com.

Pairing and local control

No Kin account is required. The app stores pairing credentials, a randomly generated installation identifier, layouts, buttons and preferences on your device. Desktop discovery and control exchange computer capabilities and commands with your paired computer over your local network. Pairing uses certificate verification, an encrypted connection, a rotating PIN and a revocable pairing token.

The camera is used to scan pairing QR codes. Camera images are processed on the device; the app does not upload the images. Pairing links carry the computer address and certificate fingerprint in the URL fragment, not a PIN or pairing token. The fragment is not sent to our website in the HTTP request. Browser and camera applications may retain scanned links locally. Do not share pairing links.

Optional Windows macro recording and shortcut learning require explicit consent. Recorded action drafts and learned shortcuts travel over the paired local connection; these features do not send their recordings or learned history to our AI service. See the Windows companion data handling summary.

Optional AI and voice features

When you use AI authoring, your text request, relevant action capabilities and installation identifier are sent over HTTPS to our service hosted on Microsoft Azure. Requests are processed using Azure OpenAI to return an action draft for your review. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information in prompts. Our gateway does not intentionally store prompt or response bodies; infrastructure and model providers may process operational and abuse-monitoring records under their applicable policies.

Voice input uses the device's system speech-recognition service. The speech provider may process your audio under its own policy. Kin receives the resulting transcript, and sends that text to the AI service when you request an AI action. Kin does not receive the raw speech recording from that service.

Purchases, subscriptions and credits

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. We do not receive your card number. Our app and backend process product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase and subscription status, installation identifiers and credit usage to verify purchases, provide entitlements, restore eligible purchases and prevent duplicate credit redemption. RevenueCat helps verify and manage purchase entitlements.

Our Azure-hosted ledger stores a hashed installation identifier, trial and entitlement dates, credit balances, purchase-token hashes and redemption records. These records are separate from local phone storage. Uninstalling the app does not delete server-side purchase or credit records, and does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Optional email updates

The pairing screen can save an editable email draft in encrypted local storage. Typing alone does not transmit it. Confirming pairing with a non-empty email independently requests a confirmation email through our newsletter service and Brevo. Leave the field blank to skip. Signup failure does not block pairing. You join the newsletter only after confirming the email link.

We use confirmed addresses for product updates and newsletter messages. Your email is not sent to your paired computer or used to grant paid features. Read the email privacy notice for confirmation, unsubscribe and deletion details. Clearing the local draft does not unsubscribe an existing address.

Diagnostics and service providers

The Android 0.2.10 Google Play build does not enable Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. The bundled Google ML Kit barcode-scanning SDK separately collects device and application information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics and diagnostic events for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. This is distinct from the QR camera images processed locally. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Microsoft Azure, Azure OpenAI, RevenueCat, Google Play, Google ML Kit and Brevo process information as described above. Website hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. Hosting and other providers may receive network information such as IP addresses and operational logs when you connect. We do not sell personal information or use app data for advertising. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service and its users.

Retention and your choices

Local information remains until you remove it through the relevant app controls, clear app storage or uninstall. Forgetting a paired computer removes that pairing, not all app preferences. Clearing local data does not remove information already held by service providers.

The current test service has no automatic expiry configured for the billing and credit ledger. Those records are retained to maintain entitlements, credits and duplicate-redemption protection. Newsletter records are kept while subscribed; suppression records may remain after unsubscribe to prevent accidental re-enrollment. Provider operational records are governed by their retention settings and applicable policies.

To request access, correction or deletion, email sweet.kandy@gmail.com. We handle requests manually and may need information to locate the relevant records and verify your authority. For newsletter requests, contact us from the email address concerned. For purchases, identify the app and order through private support; never send a card number, password, PIN or pairing token. Records needed for legal obligations, security or outstanding purchase disputes may be retained, and we will explain applicable limitations. Requests to us do not cancel store subscriptions or remove records Google independently maintains.

Permissions, security and children

Network access supports desktop discovery, pairing, control and optional online services. Camera and speech access are used for the features you initiate. The Google Play release does not request Accessibility or display-over-other-apps access. We use encrypted network transport for cloud services and paired desktop control, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Kin Stream Deck is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change.
