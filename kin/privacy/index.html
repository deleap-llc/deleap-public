Kin privacy policy

Made by Kandy Labs and operated by DeLeap LLC (“we”).

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Contact: admin@deleap.org

One account, four apps

The four Kin apps are installed separately but share one Kin account, which you sign in to with Google. Each app keeps its own permissions, notification token and installation ID on your account.

Who Kin is for

  • Kin is for adults: parents, caregivers and family members.
  • Teen drivers can join Kin Teen with a parent’s consent.
  • Children can’t sign in to Kin. Parents may add information about their children, such as a profile or safe zones.

What we collect and why

Account

We collect your name, email, Google profile photo (if any), Kin user ID, family membership and role. We use them to sign you in and connect you to your family.

Your phones

For each phone, we collect:

  • installation ID, app and version, and platform;
  • the permissions you granted;
  • battery and charging state;
  • a notification token.

We use these to send alerts and to show your family whether your phone can share right now.

Location

  • Live location: your current position, while you share it with your family. The app shows a notification whenever it shares in the background.
  • 7-day history: off unless you turn it on. When it’s on, Kin saves your position about every 5 minutes and deletes each point after 7 days.

Kin Teen (driving)

We collect:

  • trip times and places, distance and speed;
  • harsh braking and acceleration, and speeding;
  • trip scores, speed alerts and weekly summaries.

We use these for parents’ driving summaries.

Kin Elderly (safety and care)

We collect:

  • SOS and possible-fall alerts, with location;
  • safe zones and when you enter or leave them;
  • medication reminders and “taken” taps (a tap is a reminder response, not proof the medicine was taken);
  • daily activity patterns;
  • voice status messages you confirm before sending.

We use these so caregivers can help.

Kin Kids

We collect child profiles that parents add, guardian links and permissions, safe zones, and related alerts.

Kin Pets

  • We collect pet profiles (name, species, breed and your tracker notes) and care entries (feeding, walks, tasks, missing and found), with who did each and when.
  • Pet profiles are shared with your whole family.
  • Kin doesn’t read AirTag, Find Hub or any tracker network.

Camera and microphone

  • The Teen obstacle camera is analysed on your phone. Frames aren’t uploaded.
  • Voice input uses your phone’s speech service, which may process audio on its provider’s servers. Kin saves only the text you confirm.
  • Spoken briefings use your phone’s text-to-speech.

✦ Kin Pulse

Pulse learns your family’s usual patterns and points out changes. For example: a late-night drive, a late morning, a missed reminder or a late walk.

  • Pets Pulse: runs entirely on your phone. No AI service is used.
  • Teen Pulse: works out driving patterns. AI summaries aren’t used for teen drives; Kin shows standard notes.
  • Elderly Pulse: a short summary may be written by an AI text service (OpenAI or Microsoft Azure OpenAI Service).
    • What we send: only fixed, pre-written sentences such as “Morning activity was later than the usual pattern.” We never send names, account or family IDs, places, times, exact numbers or anything you typed.
    • Storage: we ask the provider not to store the request. The provider may keep requests for a short time for abuse monitoring under its terms. They aren’t used to train models.
    • When AI isn’t used: if the AI service is off or unavailable, Kin shows a standard summary.

Who else processes your data

We don’t sell your data or use it for ads. We share it only with these services, and only to run Kin:

ServiceWhat for
Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check)Accounts, storage, server logic, notifications and abuse protection
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash reports from store builds: the crash trace, phone model, OS, app version and build type. They contain no user ID, name, location or text.
Google Maps and Google WeatherMaps, traffic and the optional weather layer. For weather we send only the map area, rounded, and never your ID.
Google ML KitCamera analysis on your phone
OpenAI or Microsoft Azure OpenAI ServiceElderly Pulse summary text (see above)
RevenueCat and Google PlaySubscriptions. RevenueCat knows your Kin user ID, so purchases follow you across the four apps.
Your phone’s speech and text-to-speech servicesVoice input and spoken briefings

We may disclose data if the law requires it. We don’t use Firebase Analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing inside your family

  • What family members see: your location and personal updates, only if you choose to share them with that person.
  • Family admins: they manage who is in the family, but that doesn’t let them see your personal updates.
  • Turning sharing off: stops future sharing. It can’t take back what someone already saw.
  • Pet profiles: these are shared family records.

Delete your account or get a copy

  • To delete: see account deletion. You can ask by email at admin@deleap.org, or from the Account and privacy screen in any Kin app. We reply within 30 days and email you when deletion is done.
  • Subscriptions: cancel them in Google Play. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel them.
  • To get a copy of your data: email admin@deleap.org. We reply within 30 days.

Security

  • Firebase sign-in and access rules control who can read each record.
  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • Kin is not an emergency service. Alerts and locations can be late or missing.

Changes

We’ll post changes on this page and update the effective date.

Do not post personal information in public GitHub issues.
